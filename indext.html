About United Team

United Team was created on 14th January 2025 with two captains: Rushdi Mustafa and Jlal Mohamad.

The team played its first match against ARGHAN Team and won 1-0, marking the beginning of an exciting journey.

Head Coach: Mustafa

Under the leadership of Coach Mustafa, United Team has been developing its skills and tactics to compete at higher levels.

The team has shown great potential in various matches and continues to grow stronger.
